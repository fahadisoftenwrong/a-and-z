A quick guide to Unani medicine types

Unani medicines are named after the way they are prepared. Knowing the type tells you how a medicine is usually taken.

Khamira
A soft, sweet, fermented paste, often with gaozaban or pearl, traditionally used as a tonic for the heart and brain.
Majun
A semi-solid herbal paste made with honey or sugar syrup, one of the most common classical Unani forms.
Jawarish
A digestive paste, usually taken after meals to support the stomach, liver and appetite.
Itrifal
A classical paste based on myrobalans (halela, balela, amla), traditionally used for the head, brain and digestion.
Arq
A herbal distillate, such as arq gulab (rose) or arq ajwain, taken as a liquid.
Habb and Qurs
Pills (habb) and tablets (qurs), easy-to-take forms of Unani formulations.
Lauq
A thick linctus, licked slowly, traditionally used for the throat and chest.
Sharbat
A herbal syrup, often cooling, diluted in water or taken by spoon.
Roghan
A medicated oil for massage, the scalp or the skin.
Safoof and Kushta
Herbal powders (safoof) and specially prepared mineral formulations (kushta), used under a physician's guidance.
