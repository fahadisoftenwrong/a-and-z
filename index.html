What is Unani?

An ancient system of medicine, still practised today

Unani (Unani-Tibb) is a traditional system of medicine that grew from Greek foundations and was developed over centuries by Arab and Persian physicians such as Ibn Sina (Avicenna) and Al-Razi. It is formally recognised and practised in India under the AYUSH framework.

Unani sees health as balance. Each person has a unique temperament (mizaj), shaped by the balance of four humors. When that balance is disturbed by diet, environment, stress or habit, illness can follow. Treatment aims to find the cause and restore the balance, supporting the body's own healing.

Four modes of Unani treatment

Ilaj bil GhizaDietotherapy: the right food, at the right time, for your temperament.
Ilaj bil DawaPharmacotherapy: natural, mostly herbal, medicines.
Ilaj bil TadbeerRegimenal therapies such as hijama, massage and leech therapy.
JarahatThe surgical branch of classical Unani practice.

Questions

Good to know

What is Unani medicine?

Unani is a traditional system of medicine that views health as a balance within the body and between the body and its environment. Treatment uses herbal medicines, regimenal therapies, diet and lifestyle guidance.

Is hijama safe?

When performed by a trained practitioner with sterile, single-use equipment, it is considered low-risk for most adults. It is not suitable for everyone, so it is only offered after a consultation.

Can I take Unani treatment with my current medicines?

Often yes, but please tell the doctor about everything you take. Do not stop prescribed medication without medical advice.

How long does treatment take?

It depends on the condition. Recent concerns may settle quickly, while chronic conditions need a longer plan with regular follow-ups.
