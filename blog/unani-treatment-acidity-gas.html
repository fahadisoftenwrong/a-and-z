Digestive health

Unani treatment for acidity and gas: diet and lifestyle tips

A&Z Healthcare Centre, Jogeshwari West, Mumbai · November 2026

Acidity, heartburn and gas are among the most common complaints we see at the clinic. Many people manage with antacids for years without looking at why the problem keeps coming back. Unani medicine focuses on digestion as a whole, which is why diet and daily habits play such a big part in care.

What is acidity?

People use the word acidity for several related symptoms: a burning feeling in the chest or upper stomach (heartburn), sour burps or acid coming up into the throat, bloating, gas and a heavy feeling after meals. Occasional symptoms after a heavy or spicy meal are common. Symptoms that keep returning are worth having checked.

How Unani medicine looks at acidity and gas

In Unani medicine, good health depends on strong, balanced digestion. Acidity and gas are often understood as signs of weak or disturbed digestion (su-e-hazm), sometimes linked to an excess of heat in the body. Your mizaj, or temperament, also matters: someone who naturally runs warm may be more prone to burning and acidity, especially with spicy or fried food.

This is why a Unani physician will ask about your meal times, sleep, stress and usual diet, not only about the symptoms.

Everyday habits that help with acidity

  • Eat at regular times and avoid very long gaps followed by a very large meal.
  • Eat slowly and stop before you feel completely full.
  • Finish dinner two to three hours before lying down, and avoid late-night heavy meals.
  • Cut down on very spicy, fried and oily food, especially in the evening.
  • Limit tea, coffee and fizzy drinks, particularly on an empty stomach.
  • Avoid smoking and alcohol.
  • If symptoms come at night, raise the head end of your bed slightly.
  • Manage stress and sleep well; both affect digestion.

Simple kitchen habits

Many households chew a little saunf (fennel seeds) after meals, a long-standing habit in Indian and Unani tradition for comfortable digestion. Plain water through the day and lighter, simply cooked meals also help many people. Avoid taking herbal products or remedies without advice, especially if you take other medicines.

What Unani treatment involves

At A&Z Healthcare Centre, Unani care for acidity and gas starts with a consultation with Dr. Hafsa Patel (MD. Medicine in Unani). She takes a full history and looks at your digestion, diet, sleep and temperament. Your plan may include:

  • herbal Unani medicines chosen for you
  • diet guidance matched to your mizaj (Ilaj bil Ghiza)
  • lifestyle advice on meal timing, sleep and stress
  • follow-ups to track progress and adjust the plan

If you already take antacids or other medicines, bring them to the consultation and do not stop prescribed medicines without medical advice.

When to see a doctor urgently

Some symptoms need prompt medical attention and should not be managed at home. See a doctor or go to hospital if you have:

  • chest pain, especially with breathlessness, sweating or pain spreading to the arm or jaw (this can be a heart problem)
  • difficulty or pain when swallowing
  • vomiting blood, or black, tarry stools
  • unexplained weight loss or loss of appetite
  • new acidity symptoms after the age of 45, or symptoms that persist despite treatment

Frequently asked questions

Can Unani medicine help with acidity and gas?

Many patients come to us for acidity and gas. Care is based on a full assessment and usually combines herbal medicines with diet and lifestyle changes. Results differ from person to person.

Can I take Unani medicine with antacids?

Tell the doctor about everything you take. Do not stop prescribed medicines without medical advice.

When is acidity a sign of something serious?

See a doctor urgently for chest pain, difficulty swallowing, vomiting blood, black stools, unexplained weight loss, or new symptoms after age 45.
